# Evident Technologies LLC

Clear systems. Accountable technology.

**Human dignity. Disciplined engineering. Verifiable work.**

Evident Technologies LLC is a founder-led company based in New Jersey, United States. Our work spans
digital engineering and practical project delivery: software, websites, native applications,
connected systems, and contracting and trades by scoped agreement.

We aim to build useful systems that people can understand, maintain, and verify. Source
preservation, clear ownership, careful handling of information, and accountable human decisions
guide the work.

## Work and direction

| Area | Scope and availability |

demonstration, or public repository is not evidence of production readiness. Product availability,

## How we work

- Preserve originals and distinguish them from derivatives.
- Separate source-supported facts, observations, interpretations, and uncertainties.
- Record the version, method, tests, and limitations behind material claims.
- Protect private records and use synthetic examples in public work.
- Preserve attribution, third-party rights, and recoverable source history.
- Keep consequential decisions accountable to authorized people.

## One company, a connected platform

**Evident Forge** brings design studies and software demonstrations into the project conversation.
An example illustrates an approach; it is not a customer deployment or a promise of an available
service.

**Evident Platform** is the company's developing foundation for shared application capabilities,
resource authorization, and accountable workflows. Its civic and evidence capabilities share one
architecture and Trust Spine; supporting systems do not create separate institutional authorities.

**Evident ICU and CivicOS** describe evidence and civic capabilities within the unified Platform.
ICU work is evidence-oriented development focused on source records, organization, chronology,
provenance, and review history. Availability and suitability require explicit confirmation. Software
output does not establish legal conclusions, forensic certification, or court acceptance.
