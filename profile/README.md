## Engineering Principles

Our work follows eight commitments:

1. **Preserve the original.** Keep source records distinguishable from processed, transformed, or modified versions.
2. **Make provenance visible.** Maintain meaningful information about a record's origin, handling, and transformation.
3. **Separate facts from interpretations.** Clearly distinguish verified information, assertions, allegations, uncertainties, and conclusions.
4. **Design for verification.** Make important operations understandable, inspectable, and reviewable.
5. **Respect human authority.** Support responsible decisions without obscuring accountability or replacing human judgment.
6. **Protect sensitive information.** Treat privacy, security, and controlled disclosure as foundational requirements.
7. **Favor recoverable systems.** Prioritize portability, maintainability, continuity, and responsible ownership.
8. **Demonstrate before claiming.** Match public statements to implemented, tested, and verified capabilities.
