# Services and delivery

Evident Technologies LLC takes on scoped design and engineering work. The project agreement
establishes deliverables, timing, price, rights, dependencies, review, and support.

## Project areas

**Websites and modernization:** organize content, design responsive pages, improve navigation and
inquiries, and prepare agreed source files and launch guidance.

**Original design through Evident Forge:** develop identity concepts, editable artwork, interfaces,
and working demonstrations. References need documented rights; fictional studies must remain
identified as examples.

**Business and contracting workflows:** assess customer intake, estimating, follow-up, project
records, and operational handoffs. A proposed tool must fit the actual process and authorized data.
This is assessment-based engineering, not a claim of a finished contracting software suite.

**Digital infrastructure:** define maintainable source, account ownership, integrations, access,
deployment responsibilities, and recovery expectations within the engagement. No project inquiry
authorizes access to private systems.

## A practical engagement

1. Describe the audience, existing process, desired result, constraints, and accessibility needs.
2. Agree the scope, exclusions, dependencies, rights, pricing, review milestones, and acceptance
   criteria.
3. Build and review the identified version with representative content or synthetic data.
4. Confirm acceptance and separately authorize any launch or account changes.
5. Deliver the agreed files, licenses, instructions, and support terms.

## Accessibility and handoff

Agree the devices, assistive-technology needs, content responsibilities, and validation expected.
Include keyboard navigation, readable contrast, meaningful alternative text, and mobile review in
acceptance where applicable. Testing and conformance claims must state their actual coverage.

A useful handoff identifies editable sources, exported assets, dependency versions, third-party
notices, account owners, operating instructions, recovery steps, and known limitations. Source
ownership and licensing are set in writing; neither a demonstration nor payment alone defines every
right.

## Start a conversation

Use the [project inquiry page](https://evidtech.com/contact/). Describe the work without attaching
private client, legal, evidence, or credential material. Partnerships and institutional projects use
the same inquiry route. Existing clients follow [SUPPORT.md](../SUPPORT.md).

## Contracting, trades, and prospective engineering

Contracting and trades are available by separate scoped agreement. Confirm work, jurisdiction,
qualifications, required licenses or permits, insurance, safety responsibilities, and capacity for
the engagement. Native software and connected systems depend on documented project status and
scope. Physical and hardware engineering remain prospective; no facility, operational hardware
product, certification, or licensed professional engineering practice is asserted. See
[public claims and safety](PUBLIC-CLAIMS.md).
