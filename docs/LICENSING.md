# Licensing and notices

This guidance does not grant a new license or change existing rights. The inspected repository has
no root `LICENSE` file. A license field in package metadata or a comment pointing to a missing file
does not supply the missing terms.

Assess the exact component and revision. Company-authored material, third-party components,
dependencies, documentation, examples, fonts, logos, and other assets may have different rights.
Preserve applicable licenses, copyright notices, attribution, modification notices, and distribution
obligations. Company ownership statements do not override third-party rights.

Public visibility is not a blanket open-source license. GitHub's applicable terms and legal rights
still apply, including platform rights to view and fork public repositories. See [GitHub's licensing
guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository).

Authorized contributions remain subject to the signed agreements described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/evident-technologies/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). A PR
or checkbox does not create an assignment, CLA, compensation entitlement, or license change. Brand
publication does not imply trademark permission or endorsement.

Before distributing software or assets, inventory the shipped components and establish their
licenses and provenance. Include required license texts and notices with the actual distribution.
Resolve unclear terms with the rights holder or authorized legal reviewer. A future root license
must identify its exact scope and exceptions; do not assume it licenses every asset or any
downstream repository.

GitHub does not supply an inherited organization-wide default license. Each repository and
distribution needs its own applicable terms. No root `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, funding destination, or
sponsorship promise is added by this proposal without validated terms and authorization. Rights
inquiries: [legal@evidtech.com](mailto:legal@evidtech.com).
