# Public claims and project boundaries

**Status:** Proposed public guidance, November 13, 2026. Owner confirmation supports contracting and
trades by scoped agreement. This page does not establish qualifications or adopt a release.

Use clear status language: a research direction is prospective; a prototype demonstrates an
identified behavior; a verified capability needs a dated source revision and relevant test evidence;
an available service needs an agreed scope and actual delivery capacity.

For websites, software, native applications, connected systems, contracting, and trades, confirm the
deliverables, location, supported environment, responsibilities, price, rights, safety constraints,
review, acceptance, maintenance, and exclusions in the project agreement. Confirm any required trade
licenses, professional qualifications, insurance, permits, and inspections for that project before
offering or undertaking the work. Do not imply that those qualifications have already been verified
by this profile.

Physical and hardware engineering remain prospective unless a specific offering has documented
readiness and required qualifications. Do not claim an operational factory, laboratory, certified
device, licensed professional engineering practice, government contract, accreditation, or
government authority without evidence for that exact claim.

Safety-related work needs a project-specific hazard assessment, competent review, and applicable
inspection or certification. A software demonstration, automated test, or public drawing is not
authorization to work on live machinery, energized equipment, structures, or safety-critical
systems. Do not use public issue forms to transmit sensitive site plans or customer information.

Evidence-oriented software supports human review. Provenance and hashes do not establish truth,
expert certification, legal authority, or admissibility. Material AI assistance, limitations, and
unverifiable claims must be disclosed and reviewed. Correct errors visibly while preserving original
records and history.
