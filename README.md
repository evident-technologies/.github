# Evident Technologies LLC

Clear systems. Accountable technology.

An independent, founder-led American technology company. Founded in New Jersey.

Evident Technologies LLC develops practical systems for businesses and people who need to
understand, maintain, and take responsibility for their work. Our public work brings together
websites, original design, contracting-related software, and business workflows. The institutional
direction is one company, one Platform, one Trust Spine, and many products. Individual capabilities
retain their own purpose and development status.

## Work with Evident

Website, design, and workflow engagements begin with an agreed scope. Contracting-related tools can
address intake, estimating, project records, and handoffs after assessment; they are not advertised
here as a ready-made product suite. Read [services and delivery](docs/SERVICES.md) for the project
process, accessibility, rights, and handoff expectations.

Evident Forge is the company's design and development practice and demonstration surface. Evident
Platform and Evident ICU remain development work; public examples do not establish production
availability, certification, or a service commitment. See [products and status](docs/COMPANY.md).

## Governance and rights

The repository is public; company-authored material remains proprietary unless an express license
says otherwise. Third-party licenses and attribution remain applicable to their own components.
External code contributions are not accepted. No repository document grants private runner access,
production permissions, or credentials.

This reconstruction is a proposal on the existing draft PR #28. Brand candidates and policy
revisions require founder or Manager review. Detailed exposure findings remain private. Nothing in
this repository supplies legal advice, forensic certification, government endorsement, or
court-acceptance guarantees.
