# Code of Conduct

**Integrity in Engineering · Dignity in Collaboration · Accountability in Practice**

> We welcome constructive participation, independent thinking, technical scrutiny, and good-faith disagreement. We do not tolerate harassment, deliberate deception, exploitation, or conduct that compromises the safety, privacy, or rights of others.

## 1. Our Commitment

Evident Technologies LLC develops software, digital infrastructure, evidence-conscious systems, and practical engineering tools for individuals, professionals, businesses, and institutions.

Our work is founded on a simple conviction:

**Technology should serve people, preserve truth, respect lawful authority, and remain accountable to human judgment.**

We are committed to maintaining a professional environment in which contributors, clients, researchers, and members of the public can participate with dignity, intellectual independence, and confidence in the integrity of our work.

We recognize that responsible engineering requires more than technical competence. It requires honesty about limitations, respect for individual rights, careful stewardship of information, and the willingness to correct mistakes.

Our institutional standard is:

**Human dignity. Disciplined engineering. Verifiable work.**

## 2. Scope and Applicability

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in GitHub spaces operated by Evident Technologies LLC, including:

- Repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews, and discussions.
- Authorized contributions from employees, contractors, collaborators, and external developers.
- Technical support and project communications conducted through GitHub.
- Documentation, demonstrations, and other materials submitted for review.
- Public or private project interactions where participants are representing Evident Technologies in an authorized capacity.

These standards apply regardless of professional standing, organizational affiliation, technical experience, or personal beliefs.

Participation in a public repository does not confer employment, contractual rights, access to private systems, or authority to act on behalf of Evident Technologies LLC.

## 3. Standards of Professional Conduct

### 3.1 Respect and Human Dignity

Every participant is expected to:

- Treat others with courtesy, fairness, and professional restraint.
- Respect differences in experience, identity, culture, faith, and perspective.
- Engage with the substance of an argument rather than attacking the person presenting it.
- Offer criticism constructively and remain receptive to correction.
- Respect appropriate personal and professional boundaries.
- Recognize that contributors may work under different circumstances, capabilities, and accessibility needs.

We welcome principled disagreement. Criticism of software, decisions, institutional practices, or public policies is not itself harassment.

**Respect does not require agreement. Professionalism requires accountability for how disagreement is expressed.**

### 3.2 Truthfulness and Attribution

Participants must represent facts, evidence, credentials, contributions, and technical results accurately.

This includes:

- Distinguishing verified facts from assumptions, allegations, estimates, and interpretations.
- Providing meaningful source references for consequential technical or factual claims.
- Acknowledging uncertainty, limitations, and known defects.
- Giving appropriate credit to original authors, contributors, and upstream projects.
- Correcting material errors when discovered.
- Avoiding fabricated references, manipulated records, misleading demonstrations, and unsupported claims of successful testing or certification.

Automated or machine-generated work must remain subject to appropriate human review. Participants must not present unverified output as independently established fact.

### 3.3 Engineering Integrity

Contributors are expected to exercise care when proposing or modifying software.

Responsible engineering includes:

- Preserving relevant source history and respecting repository governance.
- Following documented contribution, review, and change-control procedures.
- Disclosing material dependencies, licensing obligations, and known compatibility concerns.
- Avoiding intentional introduction of malicious code, concealed behavior, or unauthorized data collection.
- Describing security properties and limitations accurately.
- Respecting intellectual-property rights and applicable open-source licenses.
- Obtaining required authorization before accessing protected resources or performing consequential operations.

No contributor may claim authority to approve, merge, deploy, disclose, or release work merely because they can technically perform the action.

### 3.4 Privacy and Confidentiality

Participants must protect confidential, privileged, proprietary, and personally identifying information.

**Do not submit real case records, client files, credentials, private communications, financial information, protected personal data, or other restricted material to public GitHub channels without verified authorization and an appropriate lawful basis.**

Use synthetic, anonymized, or otherwise properly authorized examples where practical.

Preserve original records and distinguish them from derivatives when working with evidence-oriented material.

If sensitive information is exposed, report the issue promptly through an appropriate private channel. Do not amplify the exposure through public discussion, unnecessary reproduction, or redistribution.

## 4. Unacceptable Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited in covered spaces:

| Category | Prohibited behavior |
|---|---|
| **Harassment** | Threats, stalking, intimidation, targeted abuse, or persistent unwanted personal contact. |
| **Discrimination** | Targeted mistreatment or discriminatory harassment based on protected characteristics, including race, color, religion, national origin, sex, disability, or other characteristics protected by applicable law. |
| **Deception** | Deliberate impersonation, fabricated evidence, falsified credentials, or material misrepresentation of work or results. |
| **Privacy violations** | Doxxing, unauthorized disclosure, exposure of credentials, or improper publication of restricted information. |
| **Security abuse** | Unauthorized access, malicious contributions, exploitation of systems outside an approved testing scope, or interference with services. |
| **Intellectual-property abuse** | Knowingly submitting material without sufficient rights, removing required attribution, or misrepresenting ownership or licensing. |
| **Platform misuse** | Spam, coordinated disruption, malicious issue submissions, or using project channels to threaten, harass, or improperly pressure participants. |
| **Retaliation** | Punishing, threatening, or harassing someone for making a good-faith report or participating appropriately in a conduct review. |

This list is illustrative rather than exhaustive. Conduct may violate these standards even when it does not match a specific example.

## 5. Freedom of Expression and Good-Faith Disagreement

Evident Technologies supports independent inquiry, freedom of conscience, and respectful debate.

Participants may question technical decisions, challenge assumptions, identify security concerns, criticize institutional practices, and express differing perspectives within the legitimate purpose of the relevant project space.

Good-faith disagreement does not become misconduct merely because it is uncomfortable, unpopular, or critical of an organizational decision.

Moderation decisions should consider context, conduct, relevance, severity, and the distinction between criticism of ideas and mistreatment of individuals.

**We seek disciplined discussion—not enforced agreement.**

This commitment does not require Evident Technologies to host unrelated content, threats, harassment, or activity that violates applicable rules.

## 6. Security and Responsible Disclosure

Security concerns must be directed through the project's applicable security-reporting process.

Consult [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) where available, or contact:

**security@evidtech.com**

Do not publish working exploits, credentials, sensitive vulnerabilities, or confidential system details before appropriate coordinated review.

Publicly available source code does not, by itself, authorize intrusive testing against deployed systems, customer accounts, infrastructure, or third-party services.

Security research conducted within a separately authorized scope should be reported responsibly and documented accurately.

## 7. Reporting Conduct Concerns

Conduct concerns may be submitted privately to:

**legal@evidtech.com**

Suggested email subject: `Conduct Report — [Brief Description]`

When practical, include:

- A description of the relevant conduct.
- Links to GitHub issues, discussions, comments, or pull requests.
- Relevant dates and supporting context.
- Any immediate safety, privacy, or security concern.
- Whether the concern has already been reported through another channel.

Reports should be factual and limited to information reasonably necessary for review.

Good-faith reports are welcome, including reports that ultimately cannot be substantiated.

Anonymous reports may be considered, although missing information can limit the ability to investigate or respond.

The Company will handle reports with appropriate discretion, subject to legitimate operational needs and applicable legal obligations. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## 8. Review and Enforcement

Evident Technologies LLC retains responsibility for moderating its GitHub spaces and determining appropriate responses to violations of this Code.

Reports should be assessed fairly, taking into consideration the available evidence, context, seriousness, recurrence, and potential harm.

Depending on the circumstances, responses may include:

1. **Informal correction:** Guidance, clarification, or a request to modify conduct.
2. **Formal warning:** Written notice identifying the concern and expected corrective action.
3. **Content moderation:** Editing or removing content where permitted, or closing or locking discussions, issues, and pull requests.
4. **Access restrictions:** Temporary or permanent restrictions on participation in Company-controlled GitHub spaces.
5. **Further action:** Referral to appropriate providers, contractual remedies, or lawful authorities when warranted.

Serious threats, deliberate exposure of confidential information, malicious security activity, or substantial risks to others may justify immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Decisions will be made by authorized Company representatives. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed and addressed where reasonably practicable.

### Reconsideration

A person subject to an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through the designated conduct-reporting channel.

Requests should identify a material factual error, newly available evidence, procedural concern, or other substantial reason for review.

Evident Technologies may decline repetitive or abusive requests. Reconsideration does not automatically suspend an otherwise valid restriction.

Nothing in this section eliminates legal rights, contractual remedies, or external processes that cannot lawfully be waived by this document.

## 9. Organizational Authority and Boundaries

This Code establishes expectations for conduct in Company-controlled GitHub spaces. It does not create independent legal, judicial, regulatory, or governmental authority.

It does not:

- Establish an employment or contractor relationship.
- Grant access to proprietary repositories, systems, or customer information.
- Authorize legal representation, investigative activity, or litigation on behalf of another person.
- Replace applicable professional rules, employment obligations, written agreements, or governing law.
- Override GitHub's own platform policies or enforcement authority.
- Determine liability, criminal culpability, or disputed legal rights.

Matters requiring professional legal judgment, confidentiality protections, or formal proceedings must be handled through appropriate authorized channels.

## 10. Stewardship and Continuous Improvement

Evident Technologies intends to maintain standards that are understandable, consistently administered, and capable of improvement.

We encourage responsible suggestions concerning accessibility, contributor safety, engineering ethics, security disclosure, and fair participation.

This document may be revised as organizational responsibilities, technologies, and applicable requirements evolve.

Material changes should be recorded through version-controlled history, with prior versions preserved for reference.

The authoritative published revision is the version maintained in the applicable default branch of the Company's GitHub organization, subject to any more specific governing document.
