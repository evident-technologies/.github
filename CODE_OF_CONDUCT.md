# Code of conduct

**Owner:** Evident Technologies LLC.

**Revision status:** Proposed modernization for Manager review. The default-branch policy remains
controlling until approved. See the [policy review](docs/POLICY-REVIEW.md).

This code applies to company-operated GitHub spaces and participants, including authorized
contributors, contractors, clients, and the public.

## Professional participation

Communicate accurately and respectfully. Represent your identity, affiliation, and qualifications
honestly. Discuss technical disagreements with concrete evidence and protect confidential
information.

Describe software capabilities and limitations accurately. Keep real case data, personal
information, privileged records, credentials, and private operational details out of public
channels. Report vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not demonstrate unauthorized
access or publish exploits in issues or discussions.

Harassment, intimidation, targeted personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, impersonation,
unauthorized access, disclosure of confidential information, and misuse of company channels for
litigation strategy are prohibited. Participants must comply with applicable law and their
agreements.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to [legal@evidtech.com](mailto:legal@evidtech.com) with the subject
`[CONDUCT]` and a concise description. Anonymous reports are accepted, although missing context may
limit investigation. Describe sensitive material before attempting transfer.

The Manager reviews reports and determines action. The company may remove content, close issues or
discussions, restrict access, address breaches under applicable agreements, or pursue available
remedies. Contractual and legal rights remain governed by the relevant agreements and applicable
law.

## Scope

This document governs participation in company-operated GitHub spaces. It does not replace
professional conduct rules, court rules, signed agreements, or law applicable to clients, attorneys,
or legal proceedings. It creates no support, employment, contractor, or attorney-client
relationship.

For general routing, see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). The default-branch version is the current
published policy.
